# KIREA website art direction

KIREA follows one maker across different visual media. The recurring muse supplies continuity, while each scene shows a different way of making. The dark site canvas, ivory type, sand accents and cobalt controls frame the work.

## Collection

| Placement | Medium and palette | Composition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero · The Threshold of Making | Cinematic surrealism with opal glass, twilight ultramarine and pale amber | The muse beneath a large folding canopy. The desktop face sits above the genuine app preview. Tablet and phone compositions place her in the opening beside the copy. |
| Image Studio · Mineral Ink | Thick gouache and oil on rough linen, with terracotta, coral and deep teal | A practical artist works a pigment print that flows into a koi silhouette. Keep the face, hand and pigment grouped on the card. |
| Video Studio · Wind Tunnel | Cinematic 3D, brushed steel, blue-green air and copper light | A runner in moving fabric crosses an oval test chamber. One broad diagonal and a visible face carry the motion. |
| Model Hub · The Cabinet of Possible Things | Offset print and colored pencil, with olive, eggshell and brick red | Compartments hold organized, unusual physical artifacts. Use a single hierarchy and readable silhouettes. |
| Workflow · Connections in Motion | Folded paper and photographed shadows, with persimmon, dusty lilac and charcoal | Paths connect through one continuous sculptural paper structure. |
| Character & Animation · The Ensemble | Stylized character illustration with clear contours, lilac and indigo | The muse and a small original cast share a physical rehearsal space. Staging distinguishes their silhouettes and roles. |
| Pixel Studio · The Rooftop Garden | Hand placed pixel clusters, jade, terracotta and warm cream | A seaside roof garden contains a recognizable small muse sprite, a greenhouse and a readable path. |

| Download · The Open Door | Frosted architectural glass, clay plaster and soft amber | The hero’s shell ribs become an open gallery doorway. The portrait version places the aperture below the download card. |

## Composition rules

Use one clear focal subject, one dominant light direction and controlled shadow shapes. Material and palette change by scene. Repeat smaller motifs only where they carry meaning. The [character bible](./kirea-character-bible.md) defines how to carry the muse across rendering styles.

The [measurement report](../website-artwork-measurements.md) and [manifest](../website-artwork-manifest.json) tie each image to the actual artwork slot. The six studio scenes share the 4:3 card. Keep useful detail readable at card scale, preserve the screenshot edges and let the interface supply the text and calls to action.
